Kuvvet alma

 $$ x^{2}=x\cdot x $$ 

 $$ \left(-x\right)^{2}=\left(-x\right)\cdot\left(-x\right) $$ 

 $$ x^{3}=x\cdot x\cdot x $$ 

 $$ \left(-x\right)^{3}=\left(-x\right)\cdot\left(-x\right)\cdot\left(-x\right) $$ 

 $$ x^{0}=1 $$ 

Örnekler;

 $$ \checkmark3^{2}=9 $$ 

 $$ \checkmark(-1907)^{0}=1 $$
